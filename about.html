About Me

Hi, I'm CJ Sutton, the photographer and videographer behind 98 Prime Productions. I am based in Panama City Beach, Florida, and I focus on capturing the beauty of the Gulf Coast.

I got into photography and videography through my interest in digital media and storytelling. Whether it is a family session on the beach, a lifestyle shoot, or an event, I try to create images and videos that feel natural and honest.

When I am not behind the camera, I am usually studying, working on creative projects, or exploring the Florida panhandle for new locations. I look forward to working with clients who want to preserve their special moments.
